07 — Questions

Answers before
you reach out.

The questions Coimbatore businesses ask us most — about cost, timelines and what it's like to build with MoksFix.

How much does a website cost in Coimbatore?

Every project is quoted to its scope, but most business websites we build in Coimbatore start in an affordable range and scale with features like e-commerce, custom web apps or integrations. Share your requirements and we'll send a clear, fixed quote — no surprises.

How long does it take to build a website or mobile app?

A focused business website typically takes 2–4 weeks, while web applications and mobile apps run 6–12 weeks depending on complexity. We agree on milestones up front so you always know what's shipping and when.

Do you build mobile apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build mobile apps that run on both Android and iOS from a single codebase, so you reach every customer without paying to build the app twice.

Do you work with businesses outside Coimbatore and Tamil Nadu?

Absolutely. We're based in Coimbatore and partner with clients across Tamil Nadu, India and overseas. Everything runs smoothly over calls, email and shared project tools.

Will my website be fast, mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Always. Every site we ship is responsive on all devices, optimised for Core Web Vitals and built with clean, search-friendly markup and structured data so it's ready to rank from day one.

Do you provide support and maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing support, updates and maintenance plans so your website or app stays secure, fast and up to date long after launch.
