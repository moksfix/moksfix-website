The challenge

LaundroSpin runs three businesses under one roof — doorstep consumer laundry, B2B commercial linen for hotels and hospitals, and a franchise programme — and needed one site that sells all three without diluting its premium positioning.

What we built

A bold, conversion-first website with instant WhatsApp ordering and free-pickup scheduling, clear service and pricing tiers, dedicated commercial and franchise journeys, and trust signals — Hohenstein, Woolmark and German eco certifications — woven throughout.
