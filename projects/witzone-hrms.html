The challenge

Growing teams were tracking attendance, leave and the whole employee lifecycle across spreadsheets and email threads — slow to approve, impossible to audit and easy to game.

What we built

WitZone replaces all of it with one secure workspace: a heartbeat agent that measures real attendance and idle time, multi-step leave approvals routed to HR, full employee onboarding-to-exit, departments, announcements and exportable workforce analytics.
